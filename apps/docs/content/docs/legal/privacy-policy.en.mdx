Ecbot plays two roles:

- **Controller** for the data of people who sign up for and use the Ecbot app (account users), and for the operation of our own website and docs.
- **Processor** for the data that businesses ("customers" of Ecbot) process through their agents, such as the messages their own customers send on Messenger, Zalo, the website widget and other channels. For that data the business is the controller, and the [Data Processing Agreement](/docs/legal/data-processing-agreement) applies. If you chatted with a shop's agent, contact that shop first about your data, and read the shop's own privacy policy where it links one.

## Data we collect

**Account users**

- Identity and contact data: name, email address, country, optional phone number and avatar. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the email address and name from that account (Apple may give a private relay address).
- Account data: password hash, sign-up date, the date and version of the Terms and Privacy Policy you accepted, roles and workspace memberships.
- Usage and security data: login sessions, IP address, device and browser details, activity logs, verification codes and anti-bot checks (Cloudflare Turnstile).
- Billing data: plan, invoices and payment status. Card and bank details are handled by the payment provider you use (today Dodo Payments) and never stored by Ecbot.
- Content you add: agent settings, knowledge base documents, website pages you ask us to crawl, and connected channel accounts.

**End customers who chat with an agent** (processed for the business)

- Messages, images and files sent in the conversation, and the agent's and staff's replies.
- Channel profile data the platform shares, such as name, avatar and channel user ID.
- Contact details the customer gives in the chat, such as phone number, email or address.
- Customer profile data generated by the service: tags (for example VIP, Hot lead, Complaint), an AI-written profile summary, and conversation history.
- For the website widget: a random visitor ID stored in the browser, the page origin and anti-bot check results.

## AI processing and automated profiling

Ecbot uses large language models to read conversations and write replies, to choose tools, and to search the business's knowledge base. Ecbot also uses AI to **tag customers** and to **write a short profile summary** of each customer for the business's staff. Some tags can hand a conversation over to a human.

These outputs help the business prioritise and answer conversations. They do not by themselves produce legal effects or similarly significant effects on a person. If a business uses them for decisions that do, the business must provide human review and the information required by Art 22 GDPR. Customers can ask the business to review or correct a tag or summary.

To generate a reply, conversations are sent to AI model providers through OpenRouter. The prompts and outputs, which contain chat content, are also sent to LangSmith, where we trace AI requests to check quality and fix errors. We do not use customer conversations to train our own models.

## Who we share data with

We share data only with the service providers that help us run Ecbot, under written agreements, and only as needed. The current list, with each provider's purpose and location, is on the [Subprocessors](/docs/legal/subprocessors) page. We also share data:

- with the chat platforms the business connects (Meta, Zalo, TikTok, Shopee, Telegram), to receive and send messages;
- when the law requires it, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our users and the public;
- with a buyer or successor if Ecbot is sold or merged, under the same protections.

## Transfers outside Vietnam and the EU

Ecbot's infrastructure and providers are located outside Vietnam and outside the European Economic Area. The app runs on Google Cloud Run in Singapore (asia-southeast1), the database on Neon and the cache on Upstash in Singapore (ap-southeast-1), and files are stored on Cloudflare R2. Messages from Messenger and Telegram first pass through Cloudflare Workers on its global network, which queue them and briefly keep conversation state. Conversations are sent to AI and tracing providers that process them in the United States and elsewhere.

For transfers out of the EEA we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision. For transfers out of Vietnam we prepare and keep the cross-border transfer impact assessment that Vietnamese law requires and file it with the Ministry of Public Security. **[Counsel to confirm the transfer mechanisms, the filing status and the rules under Law 91/2025/QH15.]**

## How long we keep data

We do not yet delete data automatically after fixed periods. Today:

- Account data: kept while your account is active. When you delete your account, we remove your name, email and other profile data from it at once; an anonymised record stays for the audit trail.
- Conversations and customer profiles: kept as long as the business keeps them in its workspace. When the business erases a customer in the app, that customer's profile, conversations, messages and files are deleted permanently. Deleting a workspace hides it but does not yet erase its data; the business can ask security@ecbot.dev to erase it.
- Activity and audit logs: append-only and currently kept without a time limit, so we can investigate security incidents. They record who did what, not the content of conversations. **[A retention period is not yet set.]**
- Billing and tax records: as long as accounting law requires, usually **[10] years** in Vietnam.
- Backups: kept by our database provider on its own schedule. **[Backup retention to be confirmed.]**

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to:

- access your data and get a copy in a portable format;
- correct inaccurate data;
- delete your data;
- restrict or object to processing, including processing based on legitimate interests and profiling;
- withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing;
- not be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing with legal or similarly significant effects;
- lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority: in the EU, your local data protection authority; in Vietnam, the Department of Cybersecurity and High-Tech Crime Prevention (A05), Ministry of Public Security.

**How to exercise them.** Account users can download their personal data and delete their account from their profile in the app, or email security@ecbot.dev. Businesses can export or erase one customer's data from that customer's page; a full export of a workspace is not yet available in the app, so ask us. We answer within one month under the GDPR and within the time limits of Vietnamese law (72 hours for some requests under Decree 13). If you are a customer of a business using Ecbot, contact that business; we will help it respond.

## Security and breach notification

We protect data with encryption in transit, access control by role, password hashing, optional two-factor authentication (authenticator app codes and recovery codes), audit logs, and least-privilege access for staff. If a personal data breach occurs, we notify the competent authority within **72 hours** of becoming aware of it where required (GDPR Art 33, Decree 13 Art 23), inform affected people without undue delay when the risk is high, and notify affected business customers without undue delay so they can meet their own obligations.

## Cookies and local storage

The app uses strictly necessary cookies and browser storage for sign-in, language and security. The website widget stores a random visitor ID so a visitor can continue a conversation. Our marketing website, ecbot.dev, uses PostHog product analytics (US cloud) to record page views and clicks so we understand how visitors use the site. It sets no cookies until a visitor agrees. We do not use advertising cookies. **[Counsel to confirm whether PostHog needs a consent banner for EU visitors.]**

## Children

Ecbot is not intended for children under 16, and account users must be adults able to enter a contract. Businesses must not use Ecbot to knowingly collect children's data without the consent their law requires.
