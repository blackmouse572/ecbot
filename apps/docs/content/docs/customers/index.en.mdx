Every person who messages your agent becomes a customer in Ecbot. The profile keeps their name, contact details, notes and tags, and it follows them across channels, so a customer who writes on Messenger and later on Zalo can be treated as one person. You see and edit it next to each conversation.

## Customer profiles

A profile is created the first time someone messages one of your channels. After that it fills in by itself:

- the name comes from the chat app when it is available;
- the agent saves the name, phone, email and language the customer shares, and other details you ask it to collect (for example a delivery address);
- after a conversation ends, Ecbot adds tags such as Hot lead or Returning customer, and a short summary for the agent.

The agent never stores payment details. To see or edit a profile, open **Conversations**, pick a chat, and use the customer panel on the side. It shows the name, phone, email, language and notes, the **Contact points** (each chat app the customer wrote from), **Tags** and the customer's follow-ups.

## Merge duplicate customers

When two profiles in your workspace end up with the same phone number or email, Ecbot suggests merging them and notifies your team.

Choose **Review** on a suggestion, pick the customer to keep and which name, phone, email, language and notes to keep, then **Confirm merge**. Their chats and contact points move to the kept profile. Choose **Dismiss** if they are different people. If you merged by mistake, open the customer panel and choose **Unmerge**.

## Conversations and handoff

Open **Conversations** to read every chat. Each conversation has a status:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Bot active** | The agent is replying. |
| **Needs you** | The agent handed the chat to your staff. |
| **You're handling** | A staff member paused the agent and is replying. |
| **Resolved** | The conversation is closed. A new message from the customer reopens it. |

The agent hands a conversation to your staff when:

- the customer asks for a person, using words such as "staff", "nhân viên" or "tư vấn viên", or the keywords you add in the agent's **Handoff Settings**;
- the agent fails to reply several times in a row (3 by default);
- the customer gets a tag that triggers a handoff, see [Customer tags](/docs/customers/tags);
- a guardrail blocks a message and the agent is set to escalate blocked messages.

On handoff the agent stops replying in that conversation and everyone in the workspace gets a notification. Reply from the conversation, use **Pause bot** to take over a chat yourself, **Resolve** when you are done, or **Restore to auto** to let the agent answer again.
