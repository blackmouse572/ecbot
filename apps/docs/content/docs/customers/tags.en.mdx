    a: Hot lead, New lead, Returning customer, VIP, Positive, Negative, Complaint and Angry. Complaint and Angry trigger a handoff by default.

    a: The agent during a chat, Ecbot after a conversation ends, and your staff by hand in the customer panel.
  - q: Can I rename or delete the default tags?
    a: Yes. The defaults are ordinary tags in your workspace. Edit or remove them like any tag you create.

Customer tags are labels such as VIP, Hot lead or Angry that help your team sort customers. Each workspace has its own list. The agent and Ecbot add tags automatically, your staff can add them by hand, and a tag can be set to pass the conversation to your staff as soon as a customer gets it.

## Manage tags

Open **Customer tags** under **Customers** in the sidebar.

Each tag has:

- a **Name**, unique in your workspace (up to 64 characters);
- an **Emoji** shown next to the name;
- a **Description**, which the agent reads to decide when the tag applies, so write it as a rule, for example "Customer mentions a wholesale order of 50 units or more";
- **Triggers handoff**, to pass the chat to your staff when the tag is added.

## How tags get added

| Who | When |
| --- | --- |
| **The agent** | During a chat, when the description matches what the customer says. Adding a handoff tag passes the chat to your staff right away. |
| **Ecbot** | After a conversation is resolved or has been quiet for 30 minutes, Ecbot reads the recent messages and updates the tags that do not trigger a handoff. |
| **Your staff** | Any time, with **Add tag** in the customer panel of a conversation. |

Use handoff tags for signals that need a person quickly, such as an angry customer or a complaint.
