The Marketing overview is the first page of the **Marketing** group in the app's sidebar. It shows how many customers are at each stage, which of them are worth a message this week, and whether customers are opting out because the shop messages too much.

## When to use it

Open it once a week to decide who to message next. Every number on the page opens the customer list already filtered to those customers, so you can go from a count to the people behind it in one click.

## To do this week

One row per stage, with how many customers are worth a message and why:

| Row              | Who it counts                                                                                     |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **New**          | Customers who wrote for the first time this week. Answer quickly so they keep asking.             |
| **Interested**   | Customers who looked at products but did not ask the price.                                       |
| **Asked price**  | Customers who asked the price in the last 7 days and did not buy.                                 |
| **Bought once**  | Customers who bought for the first time 15 to 30 days ago, ready for something that goes with it. |
| **Repeat buyer** | Customers about to run out, based on each product group's repurchase cycle.                       |
| **Inactive**     | Customers who bought but have not come back in 31 to 90 days.                                     |

Ecbot leaves out customers who opted out, are paused or are upset, and says how many it left out.

## Which stage customers are at

The funnel shows how many customers reached each stage, split by the channel they first wrote from. Below it, one card per stage shows the count and its share of all customers. Click a card to see those customers.

## Stage moves

How many customers moved from one stage to another in the date range, such as Asked price to Bought once. **View customers** opens the list at the new stage.

## Is the list healthy

- **Opted out**: customers who stopped offers in the range, compared with the range before. Lower is better.
- **Paused**: customers who receive offers again when their pause ends.

A rise in opt-outs is a sign the shop is messaging too much.
