The Marketing overview shows how many customers are at each stage, which of them are worth a message this week, and whether customers are opting out because the shop messages too much. It is the first page of the **Marketing** group in the app's sidebar.

## How Marketing works

Marketing in Ecbot follows one cycle, and each step has its own page:

1. **Attract**: see which flyer, post, counter or KOL brings customers, with [Customer sources](/docs/marketing/sources).
2. **Understand**: Ecbot labels each customer with a stage and whether they want offers, see [Customer stages and offers](/docs/marketing/stages).
3. **Record**: confirmed orders tell Ecbot who bought what and when, see [Orders and product groups](/docs/marketing/orders).
4. **Find**: this page lists who is worth a message this week, and why.
5. **Act**: you message those customers, then watch the stages and list health change.

New to Marketing? Follow [Set up Marketing](/docs/marketing/setup) first, so this page has orders and sources to work from.

<Callout type="warn" title="This page does not send messages">
  A number opens the customer list filtered to those customers. Ecbot does not
  send anything from here: you message each customer from their conversation.
  Sending offers to a whole group from Ecbot comes in a later release.
</Callout>

## When to use it

Open it once a week to decide who to message next. Every number on the page opens the customer list already filtered to those customers, so you can go from a count to the people behind it in one click.

## To do this week

One row per group of customers, with how many are worth a message, and what to do once you open the list:

| Row              | Who it counts                                                               | What to do next                                                                    |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **New**          | Customers who wrote for the first time this week.                           | Check they got a quick, useful answer, so they keep asking.                        |
| **Interested**   | Customers who looked at products but did not ask the price.                 | Send the price, a photo or a review of the product they looked at.                 |
| **Asked price**  | Customers who asked the price in the last 7 days and did not buy.           | Read the chat, answer what held them back (size, shipping, price), offer to order. |
| **Bought once**  | Customers who bought for the first time 15 to 30 days ago.                  | Ask how the product went, and suggest something that goes with it.                 |
| **Repeat buyer** | Customers about to run out, based on each product group's repurchase cycle. | Remind them to reorder before they run out.                                        |
| **Win-back**     | Customers who bought but have not come back in 31 to 90 days.               | Send something new or a small offer to bring them back.                            |

These rows are groups to act on this week, not stages. **Win-back** is not a stage: a Win-back customer is still at Bought once or Repeat buyer. See [Stages and To do rows](/docs/marketing/stages#stages-and-to-do-rows).

### Who you can contact

Each row only counts customers the shop may send offers to. Ecbot leaves out customers who:

- stopped offers for good, or sent "stop",
- paused offers, or paused the product group the row is about,
- are marked **Upset**.

The total at the top says how many customers were left out. Leave them out of your messages too.

## Which stage customers are at

The funnel shows how many customers reached each stage, split by the channel they first wrote from. Below it, one card per stage shows the count and its share of all customers. Click a card to see those customers.

## Stage moves

How many customers moved from one stage to another in the date range, such as Asked price to Bought once. **View customers** opens the list at the new stage.

## Is the list healthy

- **Opted out**: customers who stopped offers in the range, compared with the range before. Lower is better.
- **Paused**: customers who receive offers again when their pause ends.

A rise in opt-outs is a sign the shop is messaging too much.

## The date range and the To do rows

The date range in the top right changes the funnel, stage moves and list health. It does not change the To do rows: each row keeps its own window, such as the last 7 days for Asked price or 31 to 90 days for Win-back, and counts customers as of today.

Read [Customer stages and offers](/docs/marketing/stages) for how each stage is set.
