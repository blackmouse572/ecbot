  - q: Which channels can a tracked link open?
    a: Connected channels that can open a chat from a link, such as Messenger, Instagram, Telegram, WhatsApp and the website chat. Threads and the API channel cannot.
  - q: Do customers see the source name?
    a: No. Only you and your team see it. Customers see the greeting the agent sends.
  - q: What happens when I stop a source?
    a: Its link stops being tracked. Use again turns it back on, and Delete removes it with its QR and KOL codes.

Customer sources are tracked links and QR codes. Every customer who opens one is recorded with that source, so you can see which flyer, post, counter or KOL brings customers and orders. Open **Customer sources** in the **Marketing** group of the sidebar.

## Create a tracked link

1. Click **Create link**.
2. Enter a **Source name** only your team sees, such as "Flyer in the Tết gift box".
3. Pick the channel customers will chat on. For the website chat, enter the page where your chat widget runs.
4. Optionally write a **First greeting**. The agent sends it when the customer opens the link, then chats as usual.
5. Optionally pick tags. Customers who come through the link get them.
6. Click **Create link**, then copy the link or download the QR code (PNG for print, SVG for your designer).

## One code per place or person

Inside a source, add a QR code for each place, such as a counter or a standee, and a link for each KOL. They share the greeting and tags of the main link, and each one is counted on its own.

## Read the results

The list shows each source's **New customers**, **Potential** customers (they asked the price or ordered), confirmed **Orders** and **Close rate** (orders per new customer). The customer panel shows each customer's **First source**.
