Ecbot gives every customer three marketing labels: a **stage** that shows how far they are in buying, a **pause** on product groups they asked to hear about later, and an **offers** setting that says whether the shop may send them offers. Ecbot sets them from the conversation, and your staff can change them.

## Three things Ecbot knows about a customer

The three labels answer different questions and change on their own. A customer can be a Repeat buyer, have Sunscreen paused, and have agreed to offers, all at once.

## Stages

| Stage            | How a customer gets there          |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| **New**          | Their first message to the shop.   |
| **Interested**   | They asked about a product.        |
| **Asked price**  | They asked the price of a product. |
| **Bought once**  | Their first order is confirmed.    |
| **Repeat buyer** | Their second confirmed order.      |

<StageJourneyDiagram />

A customer with no message and no order for 120 days also shows **Inactive**. Their stage stays underneath, and hovering the label shows it.

### Stages and To do rows

A **stage** is where a customer is in buying, and every customer has exactly one. A **To do row** on the [Marketing overview](/docs/marketing) is a group of customers worth a message this week, picked by rules such as "asked the price in the last 7 days". Most rows are named after a stage, but a row only holds the customers at that stage who are worth a message now.

| Term         | What it is                                                        | Where you see it                     |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **Win-back** | A To do row: customers who bought, then went quiet 31 to 90 days. | Marketing overview                   |
| **Inactive** | A label: no message and no order for 120 days.                    | Customer panel, customer list filter |

So a customer who went quiet 40 days ago shows up in Win-back but is not Inactive yet, and a customer quiet for 150 days is Inactive but past the Win-back window.

Ecbot reads each conversation and only moves a customer forward. Orders count once you confirm them, see [Orders and product groups](/docs/marketing/orders).

## Product group pauses

When a customer says "later" about a product, Ecbot pauses offers for that product group, by default for 60 days. Offers for other product groups can still go out.

## Offers

Offers is the customer's permission to receive promotions from the shop. It only covers messages the shop starts. When a customer writes first, the agent always answers, whatever the offers setting.

| Setting              | What it means                                                            |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Not agreed yet**   | The bot asks the customer once, right after a purchase or a compliment.  |
| **Agreed**           | Offers may be sent, within each channel's free messaging window.         |
| **Paused**           | No offers until a date, then back to Agreed.                             |
| **Stopped for good** | Never send offers. A customer who sends "stop" or "dừng" lands here too. |

### How a customer agrees, pauses or stops

The agent asks once, at a good moment, and records the answer:

> **Customer**: The sunscreen works great, thanks!
>
> **Agent**: So glad to hear it! Can we message you when there is an offer on sunscreen?
>
> **Customer**: Sure.

Offers change to **Agreed**. If the customer says "not now", offers change to **Paused** for 30 days. If they say no, offers change to **Stopped for good**, and the agent never asks again. A customer who opens a [tracked link](/docs/marketing/sources) that promises an offer counts as Agreed.

Later, the customer can tap **Stop offers** under an offer, or send "stop" or "dừng", at any time. Offers change to Stopped for good, and the agent confirms in one line. You can change the stop words in [Send settings](/docs/marketing/send#send-settings).

### Messaging windows

Each chat app only lets a shop message a customer freely for a while after the customer's last message, for example 24 hours on Messenger. Offers you [send](/docs/marketing/send) only go out inside that window. The customer panel shows how long the window has left on each channel.

## Upset

<Callout type="warn" title="Upset stops all offers">
  When a customer is rude or angry, Ecbot marks them **Upset**. Ecbot then sends
  no offers and stops asking, and only your staff can turn Upset off. Read the
  conversation and sort out the problem before you turn it off.
</Callout>

## See and change labels

Open a conversation and find **Marketing** in the customer panel. It shows the stage, any product group pauses, the offers setting, how long the free messaging window has left on each channel, and the latest label changes.

- **View all** opens the marketing timeline: every change, who made it (Bot, Classifier, Team member, Connected app or Automatic) and why, newest first.

## Filter by stage

In **Customers**, filter the list by **Stage**, **Offers** or **Inactive**. In **Conversations**, filter by stage to find chats with customers who asked the price but did not buy.
