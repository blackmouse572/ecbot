Ecbot gives every customer three marketing labels: a **stage** that shows how far they are in buying, a **pause** on product groups they asked to hear about later, and an **offers** setting that says whether the shop may send them offers. Ecbot sets them from the conversation, and your staff can change them.

## Stages

| Stage            | How a customer gets there          |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| **New**          | Their first message to the shop.   |
| **Interested**   | They asked about a product.        |
| **Asked price**  | They asked the price of a product. |
| **Bought once**  | Their first order is confirmed.    |
| **Repeat buyer** | Their second confirmed order.      |

A customer with no message and no order for 120 days also shows **Inactive**. Their stage stays underneath, and hovering the label shows it.

Ecbot reads each conversation and only moves a customer forward. Orders count once you confirm them, see [Orders and product groups](/docs/marketing/orders).

## Product group pauses

When a customer says "later" about a product, Ecbot pauses offers for that product group, by default for 60 days. Offers for other product groups can still go out.

## Offers

| Setting              | What it means                                                            |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Not agreed yet**   | The bot asks the customer once, right after a purchase or a compliment.  |
| **Agreed**           | Offers may be sent, within each channel's free messaging window.         |
| **Paused**           | No offers until a date, then back to Agreed.                             |
| **Stopped for good** | Never send offers. A customer who sends "stop" or "dừng" lands here too. |

**Upset** is a separate flag. Ecbot turns it on when a customer is rude or angry, and then sends no offers and stops asking. Only your staff can turn it off.

## See and change labels

Open a conversation and find **Marketing** in the customer panel. It shows the stage, any product group pauses, the offers setting, how long the free messaging window has left on each channel, and the latest label changes.

- **Edit labels** changes the stage, offers or Upset. A reason is required, and it shows under the label and in the timeline.
- **View all** opens the marketing timeline: every change, who made it (the bot, Ecbot, a team member or a connected app) and why, newest first.

## Filter by stage

In **Customers**, filter the list by **Stage**, **Offers** or **Inactive**. In **Conversations**, filter by stage to find chats with customers who asked the price but did not buy.
