<Step>

### Who gets it

The panel shows how many customers get the message, with one chip per channel. Below, it says how many customers Ecbot left out and why: stopped offers, upset, paused, put off for later, resting after no replies, reached the 30-day limit, or already got another offer today.

To leave someone out, click a channel chip. **Customers who get this** lists them with their phone, email, stage and conversations. Untick the customers you do not want to message this time, then click **Done**. **Tick all again** brings them back.

### Message

Ecbot drafts the message when the panel opens.

- **Offer** (optional): write your offer in your own words, such as "10% off orders placed before Nov 20". Ecbot writes the message around it. Leave it empty for a simple check-in.
- **Message text**: edit it like any text. **Rewrite** writes it again, and its menu picks a style: Friendly, Polite or Playful. **Longer** and **Shorter** change the length.
- **Customer name** and **Product** are filled in for each customer. Type `{` or click the `{ }` button to add them. A customer with no name gets "there", and one with no product gets "the product you asked about". If the product's group has a photo, it is sent with the message.

</Step>
<Step>

### How to send

- **Send every day (recommended)**: sends to these customers now, then every day to new customers who join the row.
- **Send once**: only these customers, right now.

Click **Send and turn on** or **Send now**.

</Step>

## What customers get

- The message comes from the agent, in the chat the customer used most recently, and shows in **Conversations** like a bot message.
- Where the chat app has quick replies, such as Messenger, the message has a **Stop offers** button. On Zalo and Telegram the last line says which word to send to stop.
- A reply within 72 hours, or an order within 7 days, counts toward the offer.

## Limits that protect your customers

Ecbot checks every customer again right before sending, and skips anyone who should not get the message now:

- **Send hours**: only between the hours in Send settings, 09:00 to 20:00 by default. Messages outside the hours wait until the next day.
- **Free messaging window**: only while the chat app lets the shop message for free, such as 24 hours after the customer's last message on Messenger.
- **At most 1 offer a day** for each customer, across every send.
- **At most 3 offers in 30 days** for each customer, by default.
- **Rest**: after 3 offers in a row with no reply, the customer rests for 30 days, by default.

## Send settings

Click **Send settings** in the top right of the Marketing overview. The settings apply to every send and daily send.

- **Hours offers can go out**: the start and end time.
- **Most offers per customer**: offers in 30 days, up to 10. Never more than 1 a day.
- **Let customers rest when they don't reply**: after how many unanswered offers, and for how many days.
- **Stop keywords**: a customer who sends one stops getting offers on every channel. With an empty list, Ecbot uses "dừng", "dung" and "stop".
- **Confirm when a customer stops offers**: when on, Ecbot replies with the confirmation message, or a built-in one in the agent's language if you leave it empty. When off, Ecbot still records the stop.
