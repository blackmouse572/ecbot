Marketing works from your customers' chats and orders. Follow these five steps once, in order, so the [Marketing overview](/docs/marketing) has real data to work from. Steps 1 and 2 matter most: without confirmed orders, nobody reaches Bought once or Repeat buyer.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Prepare your orders

Open **Orders**. Confirm the orders the bot noted in chat that are **Awaiting confirmation**, after checking quantities and prices. If the shop sold before Ecbot, import past orders from a CSV.

Read [Import your existing orders](/docs/marketing/orders#import-your-existing-orders).

</Step>
<Step>

### Organize product groups

In the **Product groups** tab, make one group per kind of product that runs out at a similar pace, such as "Sunscreen", and set its **Repurchase cycle** in days. Ecbot uses it to remind customers before they run out.

Read [Product groups](/docs/marketing/orders#product-groups).

</Step>
<Step>

### Review stages and offers

Open a few conversations and look at **Marketing** in the customer panel. Check each customer's stage and offers setting look right, and fix any that do not in **Edit labels**.

Read [Customer stages and offers](/docs/marketing/stages).

</Step>
<Step>

### Create customer sources

Make a tracked link or QR code for each flyer, post, counter or KOL, so you can see which one brings customers.

Read [Customer sources](/docs/marketing/sources).

</Step>
<Step>

### Use the Marketing overview each week

Open the [Marketing overview](/docs/marketing) once a week. Each To do row has a send button: Ecbot picks the customers and drafts the message, you check it and send. Check **Send settings** once first, see [Send offers](/docs/marketing/send).

</Step>
</Steps>

## When it is working

- New orders from chat get confirmed within a day or two.
- Customers move from Asked price to Bought once on the overview.
- The To do rows show customers you recognise, with the reason they are there.
- Opt-outs in **Is the list healthy** stay low.
