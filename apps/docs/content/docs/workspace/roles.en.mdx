  - q: What role do new members get?
    a: Member, unless the owner picked another role on the invitation. The owner can change it later from the Team page.
  - q: Can an Admin do everything the owner can?
    a: Almost. Only the owner can manage roles, change a member's role, remove members, approve join requests, change the plan and delete the workspace.
  - q: Can I change what Admin or Member allows?
    a: Not from the app. Create a role with the areas you want instead, and assign it to the people who need it.

A role decides what a member can see and change in a workspace. Every workspace starts with two roles, **Admin** and **Member**, and the person who created the workspace is its owner. The owner can create more roles, for example one for sales staff.

## Built-in roles

| Role       | Who has it                                     | What they can do                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Owner**  | The person who created the workspace.          | Everything, including roles, team changes, billing and deleting the workspace.                                                                                      |
| **Admin**  | Members the owner trusts to run the workspace. | Manage agents, knowledge, tools, skills, customers and their data, usage, the dashboard and workspace details. Invite members and create [API keys](/docs/dev/mcp). |
| **Member** | Everyone else, by default.                     | Manage agents, knowledge and customers. See tools and skills without changing them.                                                                                 |

The workspace switcher shows your own role under the name of the workspace you are in.

1. Open **Workspace settings**, then **Roles**, and choose **Create**.
2. Enter a **Name** and, if you like, a **Description**.
3. Tick the areas the role can manage:
   - **Agent**: agents and their conversations, including replying to customers;
   - **Knowledge Base**: add and edit knowledge items;
   - **RAG**: upload and remove the files inside the knowledge base;
   - **Customer**: customer profiles and tags.
4. Choose **Create**.

A ticked area gives full access to it: see, create, change and delete. To give the role to someone, open **Team**, choose **Assign Role to Member** in their row and pick the role (see [Team and invitations](/docs/workspace/team)).

Choose a role in the list to see its details and a grid of what it allows in each area.

## Who manages roles

Only the owner can open **Roles**, create roles and assign them. API keys follow roles too: a key can do what its creator's role allows, so changing someone's role also changes what their keys can do.
