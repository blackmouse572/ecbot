A workspace is the space for one shop or client. It holds that business's agents, knowledge base, channels, customers and team, and nothing is shared between workspaces. Workspace settings is where you rename the workspace, change its web address and logo, manage the team and API keys, or delete it.

## Open Workspace settings

There are two ways in:

- Choose **Workspace settings** in the sidebar, under **Activity**.
- Open the workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar and choose **Workspace settings**, **Team** or **API keys**.

Choose **Back to** and the workspace name at the top of the settings menu to return to your agents and chats.

## Change the name, web address or avatar

On **General**, choose **Edit**. You can change:

- **Name**, shown in the switcher and in emails to your team;
- **Web address**, the name in the workspace's links, such as `my-shop`, in lowercase letters, numbers and dashes. Links and bookmarks with the old address stop working;
- **Avatar**, the picture shown next to the workspace name.

Choose **Save** when you are done.

## Switch or create a workspace

The workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar shows your role (**Owner**, **Admin** or **Member**) under the name of the workspace you are in. Open it to see every workspace you belong to, with a check mark on the current one. Pick another to switch: you land on the same section, for example **Conversations**, in the other workspace.

To add a workspace, choose **Create Workspace** in the switcher and give it a name and web address. To join someone else's workspace instead, see [Team and invitations](/docs/workspace/team).

## Delete a workspace

Deleting a workspace permanently removes everything in it: members, customers, conversations, agents, knowledge and channels. It cannot be undone.

1. Open **General** in Workspace settings.
2. Under **Danger zone**, choose **Delete workspace**.
3. Type the workspace web address to confirm, then choose **Delete**.

Only the owner can do this. If you want to keep your data, download what you need first, and cancel any paid plan under [Billing](/docs/workspace/billing).

## When a workspace cannot be opened

| Screen                          | What it means                                                                                           | What to do                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Workspace not found**         | The workspace does not exist, or you are not a member of it.                                            | Ask its owner for an invite, or go to one of your workspaces from the buttons on the screen. |
| **This workspace is suspended** | An admin of Ecbot has suspended it. Its agents do not reply, and nobody can open it. Your data is kept. | Contact support to restore access.                                                           |
