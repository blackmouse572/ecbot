Ecbot is an AI agent for shops and service businesses. You set it up once, and it answers customers on Facebook Messenger, Zalo, Telegram, WhatsApp and your website. It replies from your own documents, can check stock or create orders through tools you connect, and passes the chat to your staff when a person is needed.

## How it fits together

- **Workspace**: one space per shop or client. It holds your agents, documents, tools and team.
- **Agent**: the assistant that talks to customers. It has a name, a tone, goals and rules.
- **Knowledge base**: files, web pages and notes the agent answers from.
- **Channels**: the chat apps where the agent replies (a Facebook Page, a Zalo OA, a Telegram bot, a WhatsApp number, a website widget).
- **Tools and skills**: tools let the agent read or change data in other systems. Skills are written instructions for a kind of request, such as handling a late order.

## Where to start

Follow [Getting started](/docs/getting-started/setup) to create a workspace and your first agent in about 15 minutes. Then add documents to the [knowledge base](/docs/agents/knowledge-base) and connect a [channel](/docs/channels).
