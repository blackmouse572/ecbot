Ecbot gives your business an AI agent that answers customers on Facebook Messenger, Zalo, Telegram, WhatsApp, Threads and your website. It replies from your own price list, policies and FAQ, can capture leads, check orders or book appointments, and passes the chat to your staff when a person is needed. You set it up by describing your business, with no code.

## New to Ecbot?

**[Set up your first agent](/docs/getting-started/setup)**: create your workspace, describe your business, give the agent your information, test it and connect one channel. You can stop there and already have an agent answering customers.

Then use [Test and launch your agent](/docs/getting-started/launch) to check its answers before real customers write in.

## What do you want your agent to do?

- **Answer questions about your products and policies**: add your price list, FAQ and policies to the [knowledge base](/docs/agents/knowledge-base).
- **Collect leads for your team**: ask what the customer needs, save their details and alert your staff with [Capture a lead](/docs/cookbook/use-cases/capture-lead). Needs no extra tools.
- **Check an order**: look up real order status with [Check an order](/docs/cookbook/use-cases/check-order). Needs an HTTP tool for your order system.
- **Book appointments**: find a free slot and book it with [Book an appointment](/docs/cookbook/use-cases/book-a-slot). Needs a calendar tool.
- **Look after conversations**: hand chats to staff, keep customer profiles and send reminders with [Customers and conversations](/docs/customers) and [Follow-ups](/docs/agents/followups).

## From first answer to full assistant

1. **Your agent answers.** The [agent builder](/docs/agents), the [knowledge base](/docs/agents/knowledge-base), the test chat and one [channel](/docs/channels).
2. **Your agent takes action.** [Skills](/docs/agents/skills) teach it how to handle a kind of request, [tools](/docs/agents/tools) connect it to your apps, and [follow-ups](/docs/agents/followups) bring customers back.
3. **Your agent helps run the day.** [Customer profiles and handoff](/docs/customers), [customer tags](/docs/customers/tags) and your [team](/docs/workspace/team).
