  - q: When does the agent pass a chat to my staff?
    a: When the customer asks for a person, when the situation matches your answer to Hand over to me when in the agent builder, or when a customer gets a tag that triggers a handoff.
  - q: Does the agent keep replying after a handoff?
    a: No. The conversation waits for your staff. Reply from the conversation page, and hand it back to the agent when you are done.
  - q: What are customer tags for?
    a: Tags such as VIP or Hot lead help your team sort customers. Some are added automatically after a conversation ends, and a tag can also be set to trigger a handoff.

Every person who messages your agent becomes a customer in Ecbot, with a profile that keeps their name, contact details and tags across channels. When a conversation needs a person, the agent hands it off: it stops replying, and the chat waits for your staff to answer from the dashboard.

## Conversations

Open **Conversations** to read every chat. Conversations waiting for your staff are marked so you can find them fast. Open one to see the full history and the customer's profile on the side, then reply as yourself.

## Handoff rules

A conversation passes to your staff when:

- the customer asks to talk to a person;
- it matches a situation you chose under **Hand over to me when** in the [agent builder](/docs/agent-builder);
- the customer gets a tag that triggers a handoff.

## Customer tags

Open **Customer tags** under **Customers** in the sidebar to manage the tags for your workspace.
