A channel is a chat app where your agent replies to customers. Ecbot connects to Facebook Messenger, Zalo, Telegram, WhatsApp, Threads, a chat widget for your website and a REST API channel for developers. One agent can reply on all of them, and each connected page or bot is called an account.

## Connect a channel

Open **Channels** in the sidebar and choose **Connect Account**. Pick a platform:

After connecting, open the agent's page and link the account so the agent answers its messages.

## Website widget

1. Choose **Website**.
2. Add every site address that will show the widget, for example `https://yourshop.vn`.
3. Copy the embed code and paste it before `</body>` on your site.

The widget only loads on the addresses you allowed.

## Telegram

1. In Telegram, message [@BotFather](https://t.me/BotFather), send `/newbot` and follow the steps.
2. Copy the token BotFather gives you.
3. In Ecbot, choose **Telegram** and paste the token.

## Facebook Messenger, Zalo and WhatsApp

Choose **Facebook**, **Zalo** or **WhatsApp** and sign in with the account that manages your Facebook Page, Zalo OA or WhatsApp Business number. Pick what to connect, then link it to your agent.

## Threads

Threads works differently from the other channels: it has **no private messages**. Instead, the agent answers:

- replies to your Threads posts, and
- posts where someone mentions your profile.

Each answer is posted as a **public reply** under that person's latest reply or mention, so anyone can read it. Answers are text only, up to 500 characters. Each person who writes to you gets their own conversation in Ecbot, named after their Threads username.

To connect, choose **Threads**, sign in with your Threads profile and allow the requested permissions. Then link the account to your agent.

Because every answer is public, tell your agent not to share order details, phone numbers or other private information on Threads. Ask the customer to continue on Messenger or another private channel instead.

## REST API

The API channel lets your own app send customer messages to the agent and receive replies. It is meant for developers; the channel page shows the keys and endpoint.
