    a: No. It changes only the language of the Ecbot app for you. Your agents reply in the customer's language.

**Preferences** in Account settings changes how Ecbot looks and talks to you: the app language, light or dark theme, which notifications you get, and installing Ecbot so it opens like any other app. These choices are yours alone and do not change anything for your team or your customers.

Open your avatar at the bottom of the sidebar, choose **Account settings**, then **Preferences**.

## Language and theme

- **Language**: English or Tiếng Việt, for the whole app.
- **Theme**: **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** to follow your device. You can also switch the theme from the menu under your avatar.

## Notifications

| Setting                                     | What it does                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Notifications on this device**            | Pop-up notifications on this computer or phone, even when Ecbot is closed. Turn it on separately on each device.                                                                     |
| **Play sounds in the app**                  | Short sounds for new notifications and messages, sent messages, connected accounts, agents turned on, and knowledge that finishes or fails processing. Applies to this browser only. |
| **Email me when a customer needs a person** | An email when an agent hands a conversation to your team (see [handoff](/docs/customers#conversation-statuses)).                                                                     |

If the browser has blocked notifications for Ecbot, allow them in the browser's site settings and reload the page. On iPhone and iPad, add Ecbot to your Home Screen first (Share, then Add to Home Screen), open it from there, and turn notifications on.

## Install the Ecbot app

Under **Ecbot app**, choose **Install app**. Ecbot then opens in its own window from your desktop or Home Screen and keeps you signed in. If the button is not there, use your browser's menu (look for **Install app** or **Add to Home Screen**). On iPhone and iPad, open Ecbot in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
