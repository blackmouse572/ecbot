This guide takes you from a new account to an agent that answers your customers. You describe your business, give the agent your information, test it in a private chat and connect one channel. You don't need skills, tools or any code for this, and you can stop at the end and already have a working agent.

## What you'll need

- An email address for your Ecbot account.
- Your business information: a price list, opening hours, policies and the questions customers ask most. A file, a web page or plain notes all work.
- Admin access to the channel you'll connect, for example the Facebook Page or Zalo OA you manage, or the website where you can paste a short code.

## Set up your agent

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create your account and workspace

Sign up with your email and a password, then enter the 6-digit code we email you. On the **Welcome to Ecbot** screen, enter your business name and what your business does, then choose **Create my first agent**. This creates your workspace: the space that holds your agents, documents and team. To invite staff later, see [Team and invitations](/docs/workspace/team).

</Step>
<Step>

### Describe your business

The agent builder opens:

<Screenshot
  name="builder-start"
  alt="The agent builder start screen with a description box and business templates"
>
  <Mark id="describe">
    Describe your business in a sentence or two. The builder fills in the
    answers it can.
  </Mark>
  <Mark id="templates">
    Or start from a template for your type of business.
  </Mark>
</Screenshot>

Write what you sell, where you are and what you want the agent to handle, as you would tell a new staff member. The builder then asks one question at a time and marks the answers it filled in from your description as **Auto-filled**. Check them and change anything that is wrong. See [Agent builder](/docs/agents) for what each question means.

</Step>
<Step>

### Give it your business information

Add your price list, policies and FAQ to the [knowledge base](/docs/agents/knowledge-base) and link them to the agent. Short facts such as shipping fees can also go in the builder's optional questions.

</Step>
<Step>

### Try it in the test chat

The test chat sits beside the builder and on the agent's page. Ask it the questions your customers ask most. Nothing you send there reaches a customer. If an answer is wrong, add the missing fact and ask again. [Test and launch your agent](/docs/getting-started/launch) has a checklist of questions to try.

</Step>
<Step>

### Connect one channel and activate

Start with the channel where most of your customers already write to you. You can connect it in the builder's channels step, or later from **Channels** in the sidebar. Then make sure the agent is linked to that account and switched on. See [Channels](/docs/channels) for each platform's steps.

</Step>
</Steps>

## You're live

Your agent now answers customers on that channel using your business information. New chats show up in **Conversations**, and the agent passes a chat to your staff when a customer asks for a person. The **Get your agent live** card on your dashboard ticks off each step as you finish it.
