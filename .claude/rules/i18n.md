# i18n

The product ships in Vietnamese and English; more languages will follow.

<important if="you are adding or changing user-facing text in apps/app">

- Put keys in `apps/app/src/i18n/translations/{region}.json` (every region) and render with `t()` from `react-i18next`.
- Reuse an existing key before adding a new one.
- Actions and general labels: the key is the message text.
- Keep English terms where the Vietnamese reads awkwardly (skills, chatbot, agents, ...).
</important>

<important if="you are returning or throwing a user-facing message from apps/api">
Follow `.github/instructions/api/response.instructions.md` (`MessageService` keys in `apps/api/src/languages/{lang}/`).
</important>
